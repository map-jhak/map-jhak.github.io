MAP JHAK (pronounced map-jack) is a seven-person group for the software engineering module in the University of York.

Goose Patrol

Goose Patrol is a Casual Bullet Hell game where you are a student and you must dodge, distract, and scare your way out of an onslaught of geese. Will you be safe, lose your sanity?
